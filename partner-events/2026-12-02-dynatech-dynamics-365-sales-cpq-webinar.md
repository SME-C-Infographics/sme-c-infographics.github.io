Managing complex product configurations, pricing, estimates, and approvals doesn't have to require a separate CPQ application. Join DynaTech Systems for a live demonstration of how to manage the quoting process within Microsoft Dynamics 365 Sales.

- Configure products with guided selection of attributes, components, and options.
- Manage pricing, discounts, costs, and margins throughout the quoting process.
- Explore quoting workflows for direct sales, departmental estimates, and project-based work.
- Route quotes through approval processes and generate branded customer proposals.

Designed for sales, quoting, and IT leaders at organizations using or evaluating Dynamics 365 Sales, particularly manufacturers, distributors, and project-based businesses.
